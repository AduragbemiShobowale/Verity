# Participant information and consent

**Study:** Evaluation of Verity, an intelligent order processing system for building-material orders. RBS Albergoni Online, Group 6.

You are invited to take part in a short study. You will answer a few questions about how orders are handled today, watch a demonstration of Verity of about ten minutes, and then answer questions about what you saw. If you take part in the order-entry exercise, you will also type a set of practice orders. Everything takes about 30 minutes.

All orders, companies, people and e-mail addresses used in the demonstration and the exercise are invented. You will not be asked for any real customer or company data.

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and you can skip any question. Your answers are recorded under a respondent number, not your name. They are reported only as group totals, so no individual can be identified. The data are kept on the project's password-protected storage and are deleted when the project is assessed.
