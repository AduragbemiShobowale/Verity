# Demonstration script, about 10 minutes

Use the dev preview or the live site with the demo accounts (password `VerityDemo#2026`). Every screen shows invented data. Give the same demonstration, in the same order, to every group, and do not answer questions about Part II items until the form is handed in.

| Step | Minutes | Signed in as | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1 | none | Login page | Verity replaces orders sent by WhatsApp, e-mail or Excel with one portal that checks each order before anyone approves it. |
| 2 | 2 | buyer@adebayo.example | New order: add cement and iron rods, pick a saved address and a date, submit | The customer chooses from the catalogue, so item codes and prices come from the price list. |
| 3 | 2 | buyer@adebayo.example | New order with a past date, a quantity below the minimum and an upload line that is not in the catalogue | Every problem is listed at once, in plain words, before the order reaches the office. |
| 4 | 1 | buyer@adebayo.example | Submit the same items again | A repeat is caught and the customer must confirm it is a new order. |
| 5 | 1 | ops@verity.example | Approvals queue, open the order, approve | Staff see the checks already done, including any warnings, and approve with one click. |
| 6 | 1 | buyer@adebayo.example | Order page, status timeline | The customer sees the status change in real time. |
| 7 | 1 | finance@verity.example | Payment proof, confirm | Payment proof is checked by Finance before the order is released. |
| 8 | 1 | driver1@verity.example | Delivery list, enter the delivery code | Goods are handed over only when the driver enters the customer's code. |

Then hand out Part II.
